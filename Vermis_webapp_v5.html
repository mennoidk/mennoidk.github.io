Rolls

When you attempt something that has risk involved, you roll a d12, adding your level in the corresponding stat. If the die lands on a 12, you roll again and add the second roll. On a 1, you roll again and subtract it.

If you fail the attempt, you gain a point to the corresponding bar, and you must find a different way to face the interaction.

If you have advantage on a roll, you roll twice and take the higher result. With disadvantage, you roll twice and take the lowest.

Combat

When combat is initiated, all parties roll a will check, then take actions in order of highest to lowest roll.

On your turn, you take one action, and you may spend a stamina to take a second action.

Along with each action, you may move up to twenty feet.

As an action, you can use an item, make an attack, cast a spell, or ready yourself, allowing you to dodge without spending a stamina for the next attack you receive.

When you make a physical attack, you roll a strength check, opposed by the target's will check.

On a success, you deal the given amount of damage, and regain a point to a bar of your choice.

Storage & rest

You also have 4 slots of storage that can each hold an object of a reasonable weight. This storage can be increased by acquiring extra storage equipment. At the beginning of the game you gain all of the starting equipment listed under your class. Worn items and items of negligible weight don't take up a slot.

In order to regain health, you must not do any strenuous activity for 12 hours, after which you regain 1d6 health, armor, or bar points (of your choice).
